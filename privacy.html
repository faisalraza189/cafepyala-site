This Privacy Policy explains how Cafe Pyala POS (“the Application”) collects, uses, stores, and protects user data, including information accessed through Google OAuth 2.0 and the Google Drive API.

1. Data Accessed via Google OAuth & Google Drive

When you choose to connect your Google account inside Settings → Cloud Sync, Cafe Pyala POS requests authorization for the non-broad per-file scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Allows the Application to create, view, download, and delete only the backup files and folders created by Cafe Pyala POS (CafePyala_Backups).

Cafe Pyala POS also reads your basic Google Drive display name and email address (via Drive about.user) solely to display which Google account is currently connected inside your POS Settings screen. We do not access, read, collect, or modify any other files, emails, contacts, or personal data in your Google account.

2. How Your Data & Backups Are Stored

  • Local First Storage: All restaurant sales, menu items, staff accounts, and settings reside locally on your computer inside your SQLite database.
  • Local Credential Storage: Your Google OAuth access token and refresh token are stored strictly locally on your POS computer (inside the local application database and userData/cloud-session.json) so automated and manual backups continue without requiring repeated sign-ins.
  • Zero External Tracking or Developer Servers: Cafe Pyala POS communicates directly between your desktop installation and official Google API endpoints. The developer does not operate central servers that collect, view, sell, or share your POS data or Google Drive backups.

3. Purpose & Sharing of Google User Data

Google Drive data is used exclusively for creating database backup snapshots, listing available POS backups, restoring a backup upon Administrator password authentication, and deleting obsolete backups requested by the user. We do not share, sell, transfer, or disclose Google user data to third parties or use it for advertising or AI model training.

Cafe Pyala POS’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention & Revoking Access

Backup files remain in your own Google Drive CafePyala_Backups folder until you delete them from the Application or directly from Google Drive. You may disconnect and clear locally stored OAuth tokens at any time by clicking Disconnect in Settings → Cloud Sync, and you may revoke access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.
