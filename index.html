Desktop POS + Encrypted Local SQLite + Google Drive Backup

Fast Billing, Kitchen Display & Secure Cloud Database Backup

Cafe Pyala POS is an offline-first desktop Point of Sale application for cafes and restaurants. Store data runs locally on your terminal in SQLite and optionally backs up your encrypted database snapshots to your own Google Drive account.

POS Billing & KOT Printing Dine-In, Take Away, and Delivery workflows with instant Kitchen Order Tickets, itemized bills, and receipt printing.
Tables, Staff & Inventory Manage floor sections, waiters / order takers, purchases, suppliers, daily expenses, and sales reports.
Google Drive Backup & Restore Connect your Google Drive account to store database snapshots in a dedicated CafePyala_Backups folder and restore backups securely with Administrator authentication.

How Google Drive Permission Is Used

Cafe Pyala POS requests only the restricted per-file Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). The software only creates, lists, downloads, and deletes database backup files inside your app-created CafePyala_Backups folder. It never reads, modifies, or accesses any other personal files in your Google Drive.
